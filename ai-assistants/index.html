Frequently asked questions

What is OpenClaw?

An open-source AI agent platform that connects assistants to messaging tools and workflows. Mortify configures and manages it.

Do I need technical knowledge?

No. We handle installation, configuration, hosting, security and maintenance. You use the assistant in the messaging app you chose.

Is it available 24/7?

It is designed to stay on, but availability depends on hosting, the messaging platform and the AI provider.

Can it answer questions about my business?

Yes, once we load your products, services, pricing, policies and procedures.

Can it use WhatsApp?

Yes, subject to Meta’s business messaging rules. Extra charges may apply.

Can I have more than one assistant?

Yes. Extra agents can cover customer service, sales, administration or internal support.

Are AI costs included?

No. Usage is billed by your provider, or through managed billing if you ask for that.

Can it integrate with my software?

Sometimes, through APIs, webhooks and compatible tools. Each integration is assessed on its own.

Is my information secure?

We set access controls and keep the assistant separate from other clients. What the model provider stores still depends on the provider you choose.

Can I cancel?

After an initial three-month term, with 30 days’ notice.

Managed services have an initial three-month term, then 30 days’ notice.
