Privacy

This page covers the public website only. Client systems and managed AI assistants are agreed separately.

Enquiries

Contact forms do not post data to a Mortify server. They open an email to hello@mortify.co.uk in your own mail app. We keep that email so we can reply, and we do not add you to a mailing list because you wrote.

Fonts

Most pages load Bricolage Grotesque and Instrument Sans from Google Fonts. Your browser therefore requests those files from Google. The blog still uses the older stylesheet.

Analytics

These pages do not include a Mortify analytics script. The host may still keep ordinary server logs.
